# Spec v1.0 proposal: what the real-model pilots change (draft for the author's decision)

Status: proposal only. Nothing here is adopted; spec v0.9 stays the current version until the author decides. The proposals are
numbered D39 onward to continue v0.9's decision table, each with the alternative not taken. Sections marked *pending* depend on runs
still in progress when this was written (R5, RL2, TM; see `pilot/LADDER.md`).

## 1. What the real-model runs established

Every run used Qwen3.5-4B (base, bf16, CPU). Each protocol was fixed before its data and reviewed blind before and after the run.

| Run | Question | Result | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage-B pilot | Do the v0.9 noise and behaviour assumptions hold on a real model? | The one-digit log-probability readout puts 67% of its mass on the digits the instruction names; regret 2.6 coins against 0.36 for "offer half the value"; no yielding (+0.002); updating direction-only | `pilot/PILOT.md`, `pilot/critique/postrun/` |
| Readout pilot (R0 to R4) | Is the readout the problem? | Four single-pass readouts all fail (regret 2.2 to 2.5, worse than a uniform guess at 1.93); each moves the spurious mass to whatever answer its format names; short capped reasoning (R4) 1.8 | `pilot/READOUT.md`, `pilot/critique/readout_postrun/` |
| Game ladder (L1 to L4b) | Is the game the problem? | Every rung fails in one step, including "the seller accepts any price" (offer 1). The model copies a salient prompt number: just under the value in L1, the stated cost in L2 even when buying at it loses money, the top of the stated cost range in L3. Second person is better by 0.2 to 0.6 coins and rescues nothing | `pilot/LADDER.md`, `pilot/critique/ladder_postrun/` |
| Reasoning ladder (RL) | Does reasoning first make it play the trivial rungs? | Yes: 14 of 15 optimal, walks away in 3 of 4 cells where the cost exceeds the value; mean 288 generated tokens; the one miss was cut off at 512 | `pilot/LADDER.md` addendum RL |
| RL2 (*pending*) | Is that robust to paraphrase and framing? | | addendum RL2 |
| TM (*pending*) | Does the model's native thinking mode do as well, at what length? | | addendum TM |
| R5 (*pending*) | Does uncapped reasoning play the full game? | First two of eight prompts hit 1,024 tokens without an answer; the reasoning applies the right method with arithmetic errors | addendum R5 |

The short version: **through a one-token readout the 4B has no payoff-driven policy in any game of this family; when it reasons
first it plays the trivial games correctly.** Whether it plays the full selected game when reasoning is open (R5 pending; R4 and the
first R5 prompts say "not reliably").

## 2. What this means for the search

The search so far ranked designs by the item count N\* under an assumed reference buyer answering in one forward pass. Three of its
premises are now false for the target models:

1. **The reference buyer does not exist in one step.** Its assumed baseline slope (0.8 to 1), update slope (0.5 to 1.5) and unimodal
   option distribution are not what the 4B produces in any single-pass readout (slope 0.07 to 0.27, multimodal, wider than uniform).
2. **The readout is a design choice with first-order consequences**, not a fixed part of the measurement. It decides whether the
   buyer plays at all, what the per-answer noise is, and what an item costs.
3. **Item count is the wrong cost.** A reasoned answer costs hundreds of generated tokens (about 290 on the trivial rungs; over 1,000
   on the full game), against one forward pass. Designs must be compared in compute, not items.

The v0.9 machinery (kernel, cell generator, statistics layer, power search) survives. What changes is what it is fed and what it
minimises.

## 3. Proposed decisions

| Id | Proposal | Alternative not taken | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D39 | A real-model play gate precedes the statistics: a design (with its readout) is admissible only if a target model passes the ladder's trivial rungs (L1, L2 with the walk contrast, both framings) and the readout pilot's A1 to A7 on the design's own baselines. V7 and V8 are replaced by it | Keep V7 and V8 | V7 and V8 passed every readout that failed here (R2 had Spearman 0.98 with slope 0.08 and regret worse than a uniform guess) |
| D40 | The readout becomes a search axis with levels {single-pass log-probability, reasoned answer (greedy), reasoned answer (k samples)}; single-pass is excluded for the 4B by D39 | Keep single-pass fixed | Single-pass fails every rung; reasoning passes the trivial ones |
| D41 | The objective minimises expected compute (generated plus prompt tokens per item times items, times samples), subject to the same power constraints; N\* is still reported | Minimise items | A reasoned item costs 300 to 1,500 tokens; the item-count ranking does not carry over |
| D42 | Noise components are measured, not declared, for the chosen readout before any design is ranked: per-answer (sample-to-sample) variance, context, paraphrase at prompt and sentence level, between-arm correlation | Keep the declared 0.08 / 0.05 / 0.03 / 0.05 / 0.6 | Every declared value measured so far was off by a factor of 3 to 8 in one direction or the other, and the context SD alone moves N\* from 35k to 205k |
| D43 | The buyer is addressed in the second person | Third person (Concordia's frame) | Lower regret in every paired comparison (0.2 to 0.6 coins), no rung rescued, nothing lost |
| D44 | No information item states a number equal to the optimum or used as a price: cost reveals are dropped from the slope estimands, or restated so that the optimal offer differs from the stated number | Keep cost reveals in w and ν | The model copies stated numbers whether or not they are credible or profitable; an update on a cost reveal cannot be told from copying |
| D45 | At least three paraphrases per sentence and three whole-prompt paraphrases per cell; paraphrase enters the model as a random effect on the level as well as on contrasts | Two paraphrases | The paraphrase effect (0.4 to 1.3 coins in regret, 0.05 in exp_u) is as large as the contrasts the spec estimates, and flips the sign of the history effect |
| D46 | The walk-away channel is judged against irrelevant-remark and neutral controls, never against the bare baseline | Against the baseline | Any remark raises walking by 0.10 to 0.18 |
| D47 | Paired contrasts use the exact path (full forward passes) or fp32; the prefix-cache fast path is for screening only | Fast path everywhere | Its noise (SD 0.004 in exp_u, up to 0.035 in a probability) is the size of the yielding effects sought |
| D48 | The model range is kept at 2B to 9B only if the 9B or the 4B-with-reasoning passes D39 on the full game; otherwise the range moves up or the game is simplified, decided by the author | Keep the range regardless | The goal needs a buyer with a policy that pressure and information can move |
